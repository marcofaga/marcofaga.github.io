Revisitando 1989: uma análise da eleição de Collor com novos dados e modelos de regressão espacial

Revista de Sociologia e Política - 2022

We analyzed the 1989 presidential election using data from all Brazilian municipalities, testing key hypotheses about Collor de Mello’s vote with quantitative methods and spatial regression. The results show that Collor’s electoral base was not limited to remote regions and underscore the local influence of left‑wing parties in shaping voting patterns.

Faganello, Marco Antonio, Sergio Simoni Junior, e Otávio Zilioli Caetano. 2022. “Revisitando 1989: uma análise da eleição de Collor com novos dados e modelos de regressão espacial”. Revista de Sociologia e Política 30. doi:10.1590/1678-98732230e013.

Conditional cash transfers and child labor

World Development - 2022

Conditional cash transfer programs like Bolsa Família can reduce child labor, but their effectiveness depends on family context. Analyzing data from 2001 to 2015, we find that in households with multiple births, the benefit neither reduces child labor nor improves educational outcomes.

Cepaluni, Gabriel, Taylor Kinsley Chewning, Amanda Driscoll, e Marco Antonio Faganello. 2022. “Conditional cash transfers and child labor”. World Development 152: 105768. doi:10.1016/j.worlddev.2021.105768

A fragmentação partidária no Brasil (2000-2020)

Phd Thesis - UNICAMP - 2022

Between 2000 and 2020, we examined how municipal electoral patterns influence party fragmentation in Brazil. We demonstrate that the interaction among political arenas, electoral rules, and party strategies generates a reinforcing cycle that expands the number of competitive parties both locally and nationally. Candidate migration and coalition formation further contribute to this process of growing fragmentation.

Faganello, Marco Antonio. 2022. “A fragmentação partidária no Brasil (2000-2020)”. Tese de Doutorado. [s.n.]. https://repositorio.unicamp.br/acervo/detalhe/1260593 (15 de agosto de 2023).

O Voto na Bancada da Bala: estudo de geografia eleitoral na cidade de São Paulo (2012/2016)

Master´s Dissertation - UNICAMP - 2017

This study examined voting for candidates known as the "Bullet Caucus” in São Paulo - all former military police officers with securitarian, conservative platforms. Employing a contextual geographic approach, it demonstrated that support for these candidates varies according to territorial and sociodemographic factors as well as specific campaign characteristics. Despite sharing similar ideologies, each candidacy displayed distinct electoral patterns.

Faganello, Marco Antonio. 2017. “O Voto na Bancada da Bala: estudo de geografia eleitoral na cidade de São Paulo (2012/2016)”. Dissertação de Mestrado. Universidade Estadual de Campinas (UNICAMP).

Bancada da Bala: uma onda na maré conservadora

Livro Direita, Volver!: o retorno da direita e o ciclo político brasileiro 2015

This text examines the “Bullet Caucus” as part of a broader “conservative wave” in Brazil, highlighting the rise of securitarian and authoritarian rhetoric led by former police officers in São Paulo politics. The research finds that these candidates garner the strongest support in middle‑class neighborhoods and that their proposals rely on repressive measures to address public security. Despite internal differences, the group shares an unequal vision of citizenship and, at times, intertwines religious and authoritarian ideologies in its political actions.

Faganello, Marco Antonio. 2015. “Bancada da Bala: uma onda na maré conservadora”. Em Direita, Volver!: o retorno da direita e o ciclo político brasileiro, orgs. Sebastião Carlos Velasco e Cruz, André Kaysel, e Gustavo Codas. São Paulo: Fundação Perseu Abramo. http://novo.fpabramo.org.br/sites/default/files/Direita%20volver%20Final.pdf (5 de novembro de 2015).
