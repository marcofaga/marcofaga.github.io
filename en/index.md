I am Marco Antonio Faganello, a political scientist and data scientist with over **10 years of experience** in data analysis applied to both the public and private sectors. I have a strong and multidisciplinary academic background, with an emphasis on quantitative methods, statistics, and data science. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Center for Politics and Economics of the Public Sector at Fundação Getulio Vargas (CEPESP-FGV), and I completed a visiting PhD program at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where I deepened my expertise in machine learning techniques and large-scale data analysis.

I hold a PhD and a Master’s degree in Political Science from UNICAMP and a Bachelor’s degree in Social Sciences from USP. My career combines the rigor of academic research with the practical application of statistical models, algorithms, and deep learning architectures to solve real-world problems in large-scale projects — from electoral campaigns and public policy initiatives to social, educational, and territorial studies.

I have experience in predictive modeling, data workflow automation, natural language processing (NLP), geolocation, text mining, and data visualization. I work with languages such as R and Python, applying methods ranging from classical statistical techniques to deep neural networks and Transformer-based models like BERT, with a particular focus on Portuguese-language applications.

I am the founder of MAF dataScience and VotoMaps, a geospatial visualization platform for electoral results. I have developed solutions for institutions such as FGV, MIT, the University of Texas at Austin, UNICAMP, and Fundação Vale.

Currently, I work as a data consultant, integrating academic and technical expertise to help organizations, campaigns, and researchers extract strategic value from large volumes of data.
