Candidate Asset Categorization with Deep Learning (2006–2022)

Python R BERT Transformer

Automated categorization of the asset database for candidates in Brazilian elections (2006–2022), using NLP and neural networks to standardize over 4 million TSE records. By fine‑tuning the BERTimbau model, it achieved 99.3 % accuracy in manual validation.

Electoral Maps with R — Coda.Br 2023

R GIS sf

Workshop presented at Coda.Br 2023 focused on the analysis and visualization of georeferenced electoral data using R. Participants learned to manipulate spatial data with the tidyverse and sf packages, linking electoral results to intramunicipal territories and producing thematic maps to explore voting patterns and political geography. Materials and lessons were developed in partnership with researchers Lucas Gelape and João Victor Guedes‑Neto.

Historical Data Extraction from São Paulo Municipal Elections (1982–1996)

Python Scrapy WebScrap

Automated collection of electoral data for São Paulo municipal elections (1982–1996) from the Fundação SEADE website using Python’s Scrapy. The extracted data were organized into structured files for mayoral and city council races, with IBGE and TSE codes and a variable dictionary to facilitate analysis.

Electoral Data Collection and Analysis with R

R

A hands‑on introductory session in R focused on electoral data analysis for the 2014 presidential election. Students learned to import, clean, visualize, and merge TSE datasets with socioeconomic indicators, applying filters, aggregations, and statistical analyses.
